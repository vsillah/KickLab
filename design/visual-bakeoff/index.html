Visual principle

Believability first. The stadium carries the emotion; the teaching layer enters only at contact.

Runtime estimate

Medium–high: one shared rig, PBR athlete and field, instanced crowd, restrained post effects.

Asset need

Local adult rig, kit/boot materials, stadium modular set, net shader, board animation atlas.

Tradeoff

Most grounded. It needs the strongest modeling and lighting discipline to avoid an uncanny athlete.

Accessibility

Vectors pair color with labels and shape. Reduced motion freezes boards, crowd, camera, and vector pulses.

Phase 2 test

Prove foot-to-ball contact, silhouette readability, 30/60 fps tiers, and Canvas fallback before expanding.
