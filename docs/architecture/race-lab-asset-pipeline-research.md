# Race Lab asset pipeline research

Research date: 2026-10-20. Scope: read-only option assessment under a hard US$200 ceiling. Baseline remains `1d02f5465e0dec7ad1c9a722e2e12721043e6aa6`; no asset was acquired, generated, uploaded, or integrated. Prices, terms, and model availability require rechecking at selection and checkout. A listing's description is a claim by its creator, not measured KickLab performance or an art approval.

## Decision in brief

**Prefer a Sketchfab CC BY 4.0 vehicle and selected track props, with the existing locally generated path.** It offers an authenticated download API, permits distribution of modified assets inside a commercial web/mobile game with attribution, and does not impose CGTrader's extraction-protection condition on directly served GLBs. The specific candidate car is a vintage design; the same-camera art review must decide whether it fits KickLab's intended contemporary racing language. If it does not, select another provenance-cleared CC BY/CC0 model rather than accepting a mismatch.

No option has passed the visual gate. The current self-authored asset remains `evaluation-only`. Do not start mechanics, controllers, scoring, a PR, or deployment from this memo.

### License boundary: CGTrader is not a current web option

CGTrader lists a [Modular Racing Cars pack](https://www.cgtrader.com/3d-models/car/racing-car/modular-racing-cars-low-poly-vehicle-pack) at $69 and a [Race Track Pack](https://www.cgtrader.com/3d-models/exterior/stadium/race-track-pack) at $70, both one-time listings. The first lists 15 vehicles, about 11k–22k triangles per car, a 13.1 MB glTF exchange file, and 2K PBR textures; the track pack offers seven mobile-compatible ovals in 58 MB OBJ/129 MB FBX source packages. That $139 sticker total is below $200 before tax. However, [CGTrader terms](https://www.cgtrader.com/pages/terms-and-conditions) define the permitted game use as an incorporated product that cannot be extracted and require commercially reasonable measures to prevent access to the 3D asset. KickLab currently serves GLB files directly to the browser. A direct GLB URL fails that condition on its face. Do not buy or integrate these listings for the current web build without a different verified license and delivery design. The CGTrader subscription also restricts use of downloaded assets in unfinished/new projects after subscription expiry; the one-time listings avoid that separate term.

## Size, rendering, and offline plan

The numbers below are **acceptance budgets, not measured source sizes or guaranteed compressed results**. None of the candidate assets was downloaded. The current local evaluation vehicle is 376,468 bytes; its small payload does not establish sufficient fidelity.

| Pipeline | Raw source evidence | Proposed shipped budget | Mobile implication |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | Sketchfab API metadata confirms 86k car triangles and 563.5k for the complete prop pack; download bytes are unavailable without account authentication. Car description mentions 4K/PBR; prop author lists 2K maps. | One car LOD at **≤2 MB**, selected props/track materials at **≤2 MB**, ideally ≤4 MB total incremental payload. Reduce 4K/2K maps to 1K or 512 where the same-camera test tolerates it. | 86k car triangles may be expensive on older phones; target a distinct 20k–40k driving LOD, roughly ≤30 scene draw calls, and instanced repeated props. A 1024² RGBA texture is about 4 MiB before mipmaps on GPU, so texture count matters even when wire bytes compress. |
| B | Generated source bytes and topology are unknown until a paid task completes. Meshy can return GLB, FBX and other formats and offers remesh/texture controls. | Same ≤2 MB car and ≤4 MB total incremental budget after Blender cleanup and local compression. | Meshy output may need retopology, separate wheels, normal repair and material consolidation. No production FPS claim without a device test. |
| C | Kenney pages list 45 Car Kit files and 110 Racing Kit files; archive/model bytes and triangle counts are not published there. | Aim ≤1 MB for chosen car and ≤2 MB for chosen track pieces after assembly. | Consistent simple materials should reduce memory and draw calls, but silhouette quality is the likely failure. |

All three workflows must stage raw source separately from derived Blender/GLB files, remove unused meshes and materials, preserve wheel pivots, then run `gltf-transform inspect` and targeted `optimize` commands. Keep named moving parts intact; the optimizer's default join/simplify behavior can remove those boundaries. Three.js `GLTFLoader` needs the matching Meshopt/KTX2 decoder registration if those extensions are selected. Validate the exact GLB with this repo's `validate-3d-assets.mjs`, confirm no external URI/network request at runtime, then inspect the same chase camera at 1440×1000, 1113×988, 768×1024 and 390×844 plus reduced motion. Test FPS, GPU memory, fallback, and wheel/road alignment on a real phone before claiming readiness.

## Provenance record for any later authorized acquisition

Keep the public listing/API metadata and license snapshot, creator and source URL, acquisition date, receipt or zero-price evidence, rights/attribution text, raw-file SHA-256, local transform script and tool versions, optimized-file SHA-256, triangle/texture/draw-call counts, and the same-camera review captures. For Meshy also keep the prompt, model/version, task ID, credit ledger, paid-plan proof, output license snapshot and expiry/download time. Do not put an API key, personal account data, or a signed download URL in the repo. Do not upload private KickLab source or personal assets as generation references.

## Staged choice and stop conditions

1. Vambah selects a pipeline and the visual direction. Recheck live price, license, availability, account obligations, and total including tax/fees. A recurring Meshy plan requires separate approval even at $20. Stop if the source license is unclear or a reference contains third-party brands/rights.
2. Only after that authorization, acquire the chosen source into a local raw folder with provenance; create a reversible Blender/CLI derivation. Do not replace the current vehicle until the reviewed output exists.
3. Compare the new car and track in the exact desktop and phone chase cameras, with mobile WebGL/payload/offline checks. If the visual bar fails, stop at an evaluation asset.
4. Only after the visual gate passes, continue Race dynamics, controls, telemetry, full-run QA and the draft PR in the existing branch. No merge or deployment in this development lane.
