# Race Lab Phase 2 circuit family: visual preproduction plan

Status: **three-direction visual family selected; implementation gated.** On 2026-10-20, Vambah's Human QA selected all three original fictional directions from the [local review evidence](../../qa-artifacts/race-circuit-direction-bakeoff/README.md) as one circuit family. The accepted cobalt coupe design sheet remains the vehicle identity reference. This decision is limited to 2D visual architecture.

## Shared KickLab circuit system

- Keep a stable elevated rear-chase camera. At desktop and narrow-phone framing, the full cobalt coupe, upcoming curve, grip line, braking space, curb, and at least one runoff or barrier hazard must read together.
- Use a consistent circuit grammar: believable asphalt width; material-driven grip contrast; curbs, runoff, anchored barriers and fencing; structurally supported spectator and service buildings; near, middle, and far depth. Physics learning should come from visible surface and route cues rather than explanatory graphics placed over the sightline.
- Preserve Direction A's original unbranded cobalt/warm-white/graphite coupe identity across all venues. Circuit art remains fictional and original: no real venue, manufacturer, sponsor, motorsport series, advertisement, readable signage, numbers, logos, tire lettering, or copied game imagery.
- Keep all three venues visually distinct. Do not collapse them into a shared backdrop with palette changes. Crowd, architecture, lighting, geography, and turn shape should support each venue's own scene.

| **A — Highland Switchback** | Inland ochre and basalt ridges, late sun, supported terraced seating and stone/steel service structures, broad right-hand bend, dry/mixed-grip surface and red-earth runoff. | Elevation changes, braking before the bend, and curb/runoff contrast. |

| **C — Forest Lantern Descent** | Blue-hour woodland bowl, broad descending left-hand bend, wet runoff on the left, supported timber pavilion on the right, service cluster and distant bridge, reflective shoulder against the rubbered line. | Wet-line choice, visibility through a descent, and a slower entry before the apex. |
