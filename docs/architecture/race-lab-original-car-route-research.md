# Race Lab original car route research

Research date: 2026-10-20. **Phase 1 remains open.** This is a read-only source decision record. No model was downloaded or generated, no provider was activated, and no game file was changed. The previously quarantined GLBs remain untouched.

## Public-model search result: no qualifying car

The search covered downloadable CC0/CC BY Sketchfab listings for original, fictional, unbranded GT and prototype cars, plus creator listings on OpenGameArt and original-source repositories. License badges and uploader claims are preliminary evidence, not proof that every input, decal and mesh is original. None of the newly inspected results cleared the combined contemporary silhouette, finished rear, clean markings, provenance and likely mobile fit gate. **There is no 2–3 model shortlist to download.**

## Original-car production options below the $200 cash ceiling

All three are **production approaches**, not finished assets or quality guarantees. Keep input sketches and prompts wholly original: no manufacturer, sponsor, motorsport-series, game screenshot, or unlicensed concept image. Before using any generated output, inspect it from the exact elevated KickLab rear chase view and replace any accidental logo or recognizable body language. Separate wheel geometry and clean pivot axes in Blender; provider generation alone does not establish independent wheels. Record the source files, prompt/input provenance, task IDs, output license/terms snapshot, exports and modification history.

| Route | Verified current cash terms and recurrence | Rights and production workflow | Finish, wheel and mobile risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **A. Original Blender model with CLI scripting** | Blender is free; **$0 software fee, no recurring plan**. Human art time is unpriced and could exceed $200 if commissioned. [Blender's license](https://www.blender.org/about/license/) says artwork and `.blend` files made with it belong to their creator. | Art-direct front, side and rear orthographic drawings; model body shell and four wheels as separate objects in Blender; script repeatable exports, LOD reduction and glTF/GLB checks. This gives the cleanest provenance and direct control of the rear. | The earlier procedural direction failed the visual bar. A script-only mesh is a blockout; an artist must finish aero surfacing, lighting/materials and silhouette. Measure exported bytes, triangles, draw calls, texture memory and phone FPS; do not infer these from Blender source size. **Most controllable rights route, highest skilled-art-labor requirement.** |

Tripo API/CLI concept seed, finished in Blender** | [Tripo API pricing](https://docs.tripo3d.ai/get-started/pricing.html) is prepaid at **$1 per 100 credits**; generation is listed at 10–50 credits depending on H/P model, input and texture, with processing/export surcharges. Use an original multi-view sketch to improve rear completeness, export GLB, then rebuild the rear and four wheel pivots in Blender. The $200 ceiling is a **maximum cash envelope**, not a reason to spend it. Route A uses $0 cash but needs skilled finishing. Route B supports a truly prepaid trial; choose it first **only if** a future action explicitly authorizes provider activation and the checkout verifies a hard cap. Route C is a backup if B's rear or geometry fails and its recurring billing can be controlled. No provider result is accepted solely because it exports a GLB or labels itself game-ready.
