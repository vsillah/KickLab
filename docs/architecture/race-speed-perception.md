# Race speed perception

This is the default-off Endurance speed and presentation model for the explicitly enabled Race expansion. `RACE_SYSTEMS.step` owns vehicle physics, `Race` owns controls and results, and `RACE_3D` reads their current state. Speed cues never write back into physics or storage.

The Endurance capsule has two 220 m straights and two 100 m semicircles, for 1,068.3 m per lap. The course is isolated behind `P.enduranceCourse`; Circuit retains its earlier oval. At stock grip near μ=0.9, `√(μgR)` gives roughly 107 km/h in the bend. A controlled run carries about 97 km/h through the arc and 110–115 km/h on the straights. The Endurance drive limit caps engine acceleration by the tire grip left after cornering; it does not raise the bend's lateral grip limit or prevent a skid from excess speed or braking. Brake distance and Garage estimates use 108 km/h for Endurance. Coach Nia calls for braking before the bend and feeding throttle back as the wheel unwinds. Three laps must finish within 145 s, with at least 12 s at 90–120 km/h on paved straights, under 2 s sliding, and the existing spread and heat limits. The Endurance heat load is 1.05; airflow cooling rises with actual speed.

`KickLabRaceSpeedPerception.cues` maps measured speed, frame to frame acceleration, signed lateral acceleration (`v² × curvature`), telemetry RPM and torque, slip angle, and a deterministic road sample at the car's world position to bounded outputs. Endurance reaches its full perceptual reference at 122 km/h. The damped camera response is limited to 7° of FOV, 1.4 m of setback, 0.95 m of downward height offset, 0.22 m of acceleration movement, 0.018 rad of roll, and 0.09 m of suspension-linked heave. It uses no random shake or image blur.

The track builder places instanced reflectors, road seams, pavement joints, shoulder ticks, and brake boards along the canonical point list. Their world positions stay fixed as the car passes. Canvas draws a simpler trackside marker cadence. Reduced motion retains that cadence and audio while suppressing FOV, setback, height shift, roll, and heave.

`RACE_SPEED_AUDIO` shares the existing user unlocked WebAudio context. It adds two quiet engine harmonics and three filtered local noise loops for wind, road and tire. It stops nodes on pause, results, reset, mode or vehicle change, page hide, and mute. There is no network audio source.

Run `node scripts/probe-race-endurance-physics.mjs` to exercise all three cars through the capsule, `npm run check` for the repository gate, and `node scripts/qa-race-speed-perception.mjs <localhost HTML URL> <output directory>` for viewport, mode, fallback, reduced motion, and no-egress checks. `node scripts/record-race-sustained-speed.mjs <localhost HTML URL> <output directory>` records one continuous 20.3-second Endurance run and verifies at least 12 consecutive seconds above 90 km/h, a paved bend, and straight recovery. Browser recordings are silent because Playwright video does not capture WebAudio. The browser checks require a localhost build and a Playwright Chromium installation.
