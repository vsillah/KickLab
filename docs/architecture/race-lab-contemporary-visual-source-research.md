# Race Lab contemporary visual source decision

Research date: 2026-10-20. This is a documentation-only shortlist for the existing Race track. The prior Vintage Racing Car and Race Track Assets Pack archives remain quarantined, unchanged. No new archive was downloaded, no game or asset file was edited, and no candidate has passed a KickLab camera or mobile test. Public listing counts and creator statements below are **listing evidence**, not measurements of a downloaded file or proof of ownership.

## Decision

**Evaluate [FS-8](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/fs-8-a926bd251c384505b47f6e152b1859ce) first, in a local, visual-only gate.** Its creator describes a freehand original concept, the listing is downloadable under CC BY, and the preview shows a contemporary, unbranded, wheeled race silhouette. The preview's sharp, futuristic body needs a same-camera rear-chase judgment; the listing does not establish independent wheel pivots, archive size, material count, or mobile performance. Keep [Kralzfiller Oxeontrocollecto XSR Oprettòrium](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/kralzfiller-oxeontrocollecto-xsr-oprettorium-61502e501b85485dae3fa5a2cbb6aa7b) as a second conditional candidate. Its visible aero shape is more race-specific but carries more optimization and provenance questions.

The research did **not** find a third model that met the combined contemporary silhouette, unbranded appearance, source-rights, and manageable-web-asset bar. A CC BY badge alone does not clear a copied manufacturer or game model. The selection gate remains open until the exact asset is inspected locally.

### Rejected on this pass

- [Race Car 01](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/race-car-01-7243b4952d4341ff96d2c5a9ff431238): 609.9k listed triangles, a visible green claw-style side graphic, and no explicit original-design source statement. A “generic LMGT3” description does not resolve those issues.
- [Futuristic racing car “Molnia”](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/futuristic-racing-car-molnia-738a78a4c9154614b90029bf8bb80d61): creator-authored CC BY model at 18.3k triangles, but the preview and tags identify a hovercraft without conventional wheels. It conflicts with the current wheeled Race track.
- [Compact Race Car](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/compact-race-car-d597c8aa778a4f49ab1a38ba31872704): 11.3k triangles and creator says wheels are separate, but the preview resembles a classic Mini-style road car and lacks the intended modern race silhouette.
- [Generic Coupe “Azura”](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/generic-coupe-azura-2e1a3699de7e4f688d328dc005ef1aac): listed as generic, yet tagged Mitsubishi/Eclipse and visually a tuned road coupe. Manufacturer-likeness concern.
- [Generic 2022 F1 car](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/generic-2022-f1-car-885d67926c1f4397bebd4efd6b5a3f73): 172.3k triangles and sparse preview geometry; specific F1/year references and weak visual fit.
- [Rally Cars Arcade](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/rally-cars-arcade-1eac413065ef4c35b9f43ceab3af5f21): creator claims separate, named wheels, but the 20-car pack preview is overtly toy-styled and several shells evoke recognizable road cars. The 145.3k listing count is for the pack, not one selected car.

## Coherent circuit environment routes

The environment should make the selected car feel like it belongs on a **recognizable, contemporary closed circuit**, with the road, curbs, runoff, barriers, pit/stand silhouettes, terrain, horizon and atmosphere sharing one art direction. The existing procedural path can guide geometry; its present styling is a temporary evaluation surface. None of these routes imports a real venue, series, sponsor or manufacturer identity.

| Rank and route | Source rights and art direction | Build and mobile plan | Risk / decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1 — Authored modular circuit + [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/license) CC0 materials/lighting** | Shape the existing path into a locally authored Blender spline track with banking, runoff, painted curbs, seams, drains, tire/safety barriers, fencing, service structures and restrained grandstand silhouettes. Use [Worn Asphalt](https://polyhaven.com/a/worn_asphalt) by Amal Kumar as a texture *source* and [Suburban Field 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/suburban_field_01) by Jacopo Voltolina as an open-sky lighting/horizon reference; both inherit Poly Haven's CC0 rule. Inspect frames/textures for stray marks before baking. Author a coherent palette and weather/lighting presets rather than placing unrelated props. | Blender CLI creates reusable modules and LODs; glTF Transform inspects/optimizes static GLB. Atlas or reuse 512–1K road, curb and barrier textures, bake a small sky/ambient solution rather than ship a full-resolution HDRI. Instance repeated fence posts, barrier sections and stands; cull distant objects and use terrain/structure silhouettes. Proposed **incremental** ≤2 MB environment payload and ≤30 draw calls are acceptance targets, not measured results. Keep all runtime URLs local; Canvas fallback retains the current simplified track with readable lane/curb/horizon cues. | Most art labor, but strongest provenance and style control. A PBR source alone will not create the missing circuit composition. Pass only when desktop/phone same-camera images visibly gain depth, landmarks and speed cues without hiding road or car. |
| **2 — [Kenney Racing Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/racing-kit) CC0 geometry as an editable structural kit** | The official page lists 110 3D files, CC0 and a free download path without donating. Select only neutral track/barrier/stand-like modules, reshape and retexture in Blender using the same scene palette and locally authored landscape; avoid keeping the kit's toy styling. Kenney's page does not publish per-object triangle, texture or archive-size counts. | Source files stay local; prune and instance repeating pieces, make low-detail distant versions, combine static materials and export one or a few GLBs. Treat as a blockout or source for heavy re-art direction, not a ready-made premium scene. |
| **3 — Fully authored low-texture circuit variant** | Make road, curb, barriers, safety fence, pits and horizon procedurally in Blender with authored color/roughness and geometry detail; use no third-party environment model. Add an original distant landscape/sky and a controlled overcast or dusk lighting variant. This has the simplest provenance and can match either car's surface language precisely. | CLI-generated glTF, few shared materials, instanced modules and LOD/culling should reduce texture memory and network payload. Measure draw calls, bytes and frame pacing; a clean low-texture scene can still be visually thin. Preserve the Canvas fallback. | Safest rights path, highest chance of repeating the rejected procedural look unless design and same-camera art review show meaningful depth, material contrast and atmosphere. |

For route 1, Poly Haven confirms that its assets are original staff or directly contributed work and all are CC0; the source pages offer resolution choices. **Do not ship the 16K asphalt or a full-size HDRI simply because the source provides them.** Bake/downsample only as much as the approved local scene needs, then record the raw and derived source IDs and hashes. Any later third-party modular track pack needs its own author, CC0/CC BY, direct-download and full mesh/texture-name audit; the quarantined pack's Fanatec/Pirelli/Rolex names rule it out as-is.

## Gates and exact next scope

1. **Phase 1 — Vehicle visual gate.** With specific authorization for model `a926bd251c384505b47f6e152b1859ce` only, snapshot the listing/license, use the free authenticated direct download, quarantine the raw archive, hash it and inspect rights/markings/geometry. Derive a reversible local car-only GLB and compare the exact Race rear-chase camera at desktop, tablet and narrow phone widths. Record bytes, triangles, texture memory, draw calls, wheel structure, lighting readability, and real-device WebGL behavior where available. **No gameplay replacement or dynamics work.** If provenance or visual fit fails, stop and report before trying rank 2.
2. **Phase 2 — Environment visual gate.** After the car passes, create an art-direction sheet and a local circuit slice using route 1, with route 2/3 as controlled alternatives. Inspect the same camera and responsive views, scene interaction, fallback, offline network requests, payload and frame pacing. Do not call it ready based on asset acquisition, a static render or test counts.
3. **Phase 3 — Dynamics/controllers.** Start vehicle motion, handling, controllers and race flow only after both visual gates pass and the required implementation scope is separately authorized. Keep the existing Fight/Race/Kick modes and `impactlab.v1` / `impactlab.nick` compatibility intact.

If Vambah wants the next bounded action, the exact continuation is: **“Authorize Phase 1 visual-only download and local evaluation of Sketchfab FS-8 model `a926bd251c384505b47f6e152b1859ce` under its listed CC BY 4.0 license. Keep the raw archive quarantined, inspect rights and branding, and stop before game integration, Phase 2, dynamics, PR, push, merge or deploy. Stop and report if the license, source ownership, markings or visual fit is unclear.”** This phrase authorizes one model and one local evaluation only; it does not authorize a fallback download.
