## 1. Commercial scope and product contract

- **Product:** one paid-upfront, offline-first sports-math game for ages **9–15**, with **Fight, Race, and Kick** in V1. Race is the hero experience in product presentation; Fight and Kick must complete the same basic release and safety gates. Pricing amount, territories, and launch date are undecided.
- **Included:** packaged local assets, playable lab rounds, local profiles/progress, text Coach guidance, device-local accessibility and sound/haptics as qualified. Preserve `impactlab.v1`, `impactlab.nick`, and `kicklab.profiles.v1` compatibility. No migration may silently erase a child's saved work.
- **Explicit V1 exclusions:** ads, in-app purchases, behavioral analytics, accounts, chat, uploads, public matchmaking, public leaderboards, cloud sync, and spoken Coach audio. No third-party ad/analytics SDK or child-data transport. A child must not need an account, contact detail, network connection, or purchase beyond the storefront's upfront transaction to play.
- **Boundary to resolve in the build:** the current Race UI includes local Couch Duel and code/QR-based Quick Challenge/Private Party surfaces (`src/index.html`, `src/game/50d-race-private-party-ui.js`). Private Party camera scanning was removed in `0e4af0b8`; verify the shipped binary permits only offline QR display and paste-code transfer, with no camera permission or OS sharing. Do not call these public matchmaking. The dormant Live Room is visibly disabled; verify that this remains true in the shipped binary. Race highlights retain in-app Replay; OS Share and file Download are absent in the current fresh web package. Verify the final native binary and declarations.
- **Release identity:** `com.amadutown.kicklab` is provisional in `capacitor.config.json`; changing it needs a decision before store records or signing. The accepted packaging route is Capacitor 8, per [ADR 0001](../decisions/0001-mobile-packaging.md). A web-view shell is not itself evidence of native quality.

## 4. Privacy, child safety, support, and store requirements

The intended data model is device-local nickname, profile/progress, settings, and game results under the documented keys. Audit actual fields, retention, backups, crash reporting, clipboard/share/QR behavior, OS services, and native SDK traffic before claiming “no data collected.” Do not put raw profile data in support tickets, logs, screenshots, analytics, challenge codes, or store-review attachments. Provide an in-app **clear local data** path with confirmation and a documented uninstall/reinstall expectation; test that deletion covers all legacy/current keys and cached data without erasing unrelated app data. Keep data only while the app is installed or until the user clears it, subject to platform backup behavior that must be checked and disabled or disclosed. No cloud deletion workflow is needed if no account or off-device data exists, but re-evaluate if that changes.

Publish an accurate privacy policy reachable in app and from each store listing. It must identify the legal developer, local data, any actual collection/sharing, purpose, retention/deletion, guardian contact, and how to request help. Define a support inbox/process that accepts a problem description and optional redacted device/build details, never requests a child's name, precise location, raw save, or screenshot containing personal data by default, and has a documented response/erasure workflow. A support link or external site accessible to children requires a Kids-category parental-gate decision on iOS. Review applicable child privacy law with counsel before commercialization; storefront compliance alone is not a legal conclusion.

Age **9–15** crosses Apple Kids category's **9–11** band and Google's **9–12** plus **13–15** bands. Product owner must decide whether Apple “Made for Kids” is appropriate for the primary audience; do not assume a 9–15 target automatically fits its 9–11 category. If selected, apply its continuing rules and parental gates to external links/purchase opportunities. If not selected, avoid “For Kids/Children” metadata claims prohibited outside the Kids Category. In Play Console, declare both applicable age groups, content rating, no ads, and Data safety truthfully. Complete Apple age-rating and Play IARC questionnaires based on actual Fight content, social/share features, and media, rather than the target age alone. Paid upfront does not create an in-app purchase permission.
