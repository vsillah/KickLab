Zero requests outside `127.0.0.1:4173` and zero remote destinations are required across normal play, offline launch, denied network, failed media, malformed code, restart, and result/replay paths. Verify zero camera/microphone/location prompts, no analytics/ad SDK, and no account or cloud path.
