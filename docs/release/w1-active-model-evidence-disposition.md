## Method and shared limits

I reconciled each GLB's SHA-256 and byte count with its manifest and the [exact-file schedule](phase2-wave1-internal-commercial-decision-schedule.csv). I read the GLB JSON chunk for generator, node, material, embedded-image, texture, extension and external-URI inventory; inspected the retained Blender source pointers, generator scripts, introducing commits and checked-in Garage renders; and traced consumers through the vehicle catalog, Race renderer, cinematic effects, preflight manifests and [recapture map](runtime-rights-recapture-map.csv). A GLB URI scan cannot establish the origin of mesh coordinates, visual ideas or baked pixels. A Blender generator string cannot identify the human creator or prove assignment. A same-camera render can reveal apparent marks and silhouette, but cannot establish freedom from vehicle trade dress. No external design comparison or independent legal finding was performed.

The checked-in thumbnails for the five cars show fictional competition coupes or open-body racers, shared wing/splitter/wheel vocabulary, and no readable maker logo, sponsor, number or tire lettering at thumbnail scale. This is an **observation from `src/assets/garage/roster/v1/*.webp`**, not an exact-GLB visual clearance. No personal likeness appears in the model-node or rendered-car review; a full scene/capture may include a separate driver. Paid redistribution of the six model hashes is still held by the existing decision packet.

## Dependency if any model moves to replace

Use a new, explicitly fictional, unbranded local model with documented reference inventory, source/creator chain, four wheel pivots and `VehicleRoot`/`Body` contract; preserve the stable catalog ID and local profile mapping while changing its asset URL only through a separate product lane. The replacement needs a new exact hash, per-asset manifest and validated mobile budget. For Race internals, preserve named `chassis`, `drivetrain`, `suspension`, `brakes`, `cooling` systems and the cinematic visibility contract; review its relationship to every car shell.

1. **H07 and selection:** regenerate only the affected `src/assets/garage/roster/v1/<id>.webp` thumbnail after a car replacement; rebind `src/shared/race-vehicle-catalog.js`, Garage, profile restore, locked/prototype/mastery selection and fallback to Cobalt. If Cobalt changes, recheck it as the default and missing-ID fallback. Reconcile `docs/release/shipped-file-rights.csv` and every applicable exact-hash plan row. For internals, check whether any H07 image visibly uses the cutaway before ordering recapture.
2. **H11 preview and preflight:** identify every pair in `src/media/race-preflight/{arrival-,venue-arrival-,venue-,}manifest.json` and `manifest.json` that names the changed car; recapture both video and poster for each affected venue/mode. An internals replacement requires frame-level inspection of all Race cinematics and recapture of every pair showing it. Recheck Home Race preview only if its frame inventory includes the changed model or internals. Bind source revision, scene/model/texture/venue/effect/sound inputs, source frames, encoded hashes, duration and frame count to each new pair.
3. **Runtime and package:** validate the 3D asset contract, selected setup, physics/collision/wheel pivots, Garage and preflight at phone/tablet/desktop, reduced motion and Canvas fallback, missing/corrupt model behavior, offline readiness and zero runtime egress. Verify the multiplayer contract transmits stable catalog IDs and authoritative setup/results rather than an asset hash or changed art-specific physics; exercise peer join/rejoin and unsupported-ID fallback. Rebuild web/mobile packages and ledger, prove obsolete bytes are absent and unrelated assets remain byte-identical. No native project, store action or distribution follows from this evidence lane.
