## Generation and export

Ready-frame art direction: “Create one original fictional Race Coach portrait for an ages 9–15 motorsport math game. Match the supplied driver portrait's premium semi-real stylized 3D game-character finish, dimensional facial planes, controlled skin and hair detail, restrained specular light, cool paddock key light and subtle warm rim. Keep the established Coach identity, characteristic hair, complexion, age and unbranded racing jacket from the supplied v1 reference. Square eye-level head-and-shoulders crop; face readable at 48 px. True transparent background and clean edges. No text, logo, watermark, real-person likeness, caricature, photorealism or extra subject.” Each Coach used its own v1 sheet as the identity reference. Expression edits requested the same face, hair, jacket, lighting, crop, transparent edges and pose, with only a restrained facial change: a pleased smile for celebrate, supportive concern for concern, attentive concentration for focus, or calm reassurance for encourage. These edits were visually reviewed as five-frame contact sheets in `qa-artifacts/profile-avatars/coach-candidates/`.

Each source was resized to 256×256 with FFmpeg. Five frames were assembled left to right without synthetic interpolation, producing a 1280×256 sheet. `cwebp -q 82 -alpha_q 90 -m 6` produced the four runtime sheets in `src/assets/coaches/v2/`. The sheets use local relative URLs and total about 273 KB. Prior v1 sheets are retained in `docs/art-archive/coaches/v1/` for provenance, outside the runtime package.
